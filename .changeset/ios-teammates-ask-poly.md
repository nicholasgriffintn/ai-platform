The iPhone app now knows your teammates. Open Teammates from the list menu to see who you are talking with, including groups and unread threads, or start a conversation with a teammate you have not spoken to yet. Replies in a teammate's thread now go to that teammate rather than to a plain model.

Ask Poly is now a Siri and Shortcuts action. Say "Ask Poly in Polychat", or add the action to a shortcut and turn on Show in Share Sheet to send links and text straight to Poly's own thread.
