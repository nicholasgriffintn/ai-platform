Teammates now carry your corrections into every run. When you have told a teammate to stop doing something, or to do it differently, that rule is placed in front of it on its next task, delegation, routine or channel reply, most repeated first and with your own words beside it. Rules that apply to one teammate stay with that teammate, and a rule only changes how it works, never what it is allowed to do.
