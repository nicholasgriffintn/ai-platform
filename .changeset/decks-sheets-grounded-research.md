Poly can now make slide decks and spreadsheets, not just documents. Ask for a deck or a sheet and it lands in Files, where you can flip through the slides, switch between tables, revise it in conversation and download it as Markdown or CSV. Spreadsheet downloads are safe to open, since text that looks like a formula is kept as text.

Research now checks your own Sources and synced accounts alongside the web, and shows what it found from them beside the report. Your passages stay with Polychat and are never sent to the research provider.
