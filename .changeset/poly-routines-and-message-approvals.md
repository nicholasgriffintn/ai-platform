Poly can now run routines for you. Open Routines in the From Poly section of Discover and turn on Morning brief, Inbox triage, Meeting prep, Follow-ups or Week ahead, each at a time you choose. Every routine has a fixed set of sources and steps, reads and drafts on its own, and never sends anything.

Each routine leaves its brief in Discover and reaches out on the channels you have connected to Poly. When a routine needs a decision, Poly sends what it wants to do and a one-time code. Reply `APPROVE` or `REJECT` with that code from Slack, Telegram or SMS, and only the verified sender it went to can use it. Codes work once and expire within six hours. Anything else you send stays an ordinary message.

Other teammates now have the same autonomy dial as Poly, on their context page, and can earn "Always allow here" for repeated writes to one place. Sending, spending, deleting, credentials and exports always ask. Connecting a credential can only be approved in Polychat.
