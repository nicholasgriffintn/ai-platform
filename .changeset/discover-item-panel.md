Discover items now open in one panel beside the feed, so you keep your place. The panel shows what kind of item it is, steps to the previous or next item with the arrows, and closes with Esc. The item sits on top with three questions to ask about it; once you ask, it shrinks to a compact row (a mini player for episodes and The Perch) so the conversation has room, and Show brings it back. The composer in the panel is scoped to the item, and you can remove its context chip to ask something general instead. Cards fly into the panel as it opens, and the feed's composer steps aside while it's open.
