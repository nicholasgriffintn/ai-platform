Your Polychat pet now lives on Apple Watch. Tap it at the top of the watch app to spin it with the Digital Crown, where each pet has its own feel, from Sprocket's chunky ratchet to Wisp's silent glide. It shows what Polychat is doing, links straight to whatever needs you, and cheers when work clears. A new complication puts it on your watch face.

Approvals in the watch app now ask for a full turn of the crown, so a stray tap can't approve anything there.

Change your pet, crown feel, buzz and wind to approve from Options on the watch, or from the pet row in Settings on iPhone. The two stay in sync. Your pet now also appears on the Home card, in widgets and in Live Activities on iPhone, and follows the Animate your pet setting from web and desktop.
