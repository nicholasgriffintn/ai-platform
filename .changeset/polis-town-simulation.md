Polis is a new app where you run a simulated town of up to 50,000 people. Everyone has a job, a home, a family and something they want from life, and a decision model judges how each kind of resident reacts. Write laws in plain English, poll the town before you enact them, set taxes and welfare with sliders, throw recessions and floods at it, and fork the town to compare two futures side by side. Click anyone to follow them around town and read their life story. Every town is recorded as it runs, so you can come back later, step through it by the hour, day, month or year, and carry on from any moment. Share a town with a public link and anyone can watch it back, or download the recording and open it again later.
