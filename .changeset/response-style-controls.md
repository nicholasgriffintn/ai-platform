Choose how the assistant responds without writing a prompt.

- **Style controls.** Personalised responses now has dropdowns for base style and tone, warmth, enthusiasm, emoji, and headers and lists. Your choices are turned into response instructions for you.
- **Write your own.** Select "Customise instructions" to see and edit the instructions your choices produce, or to write your own from scratch. "Use style controls" switches back.
- **Existing instructions are kept.** If you had already written your own traits or preferences, they open in the editor as before.
