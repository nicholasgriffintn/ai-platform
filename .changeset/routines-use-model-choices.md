Routines and teammates now run on your model choices instead of a built-in default model. Discover's morning and evening editions use your Writing choice, and every other routine, delegation, wake and channel reply from a teammate without its own model uses the new Teammates and routines choice. Both start on Workers AI models and can be changed in Model choices.

Polychat no longer picks a default model anywhere on your behalf. An empty model picker means your chosen tier, and browser use runs on the conversation's own OpenAI model.
