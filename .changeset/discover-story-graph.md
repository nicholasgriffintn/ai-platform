Stories in Discover now show quotes, people and what came before. Quotes appear with the speaker, their role and the outlet that carried them, and only when the words appear exactly in the article. People and organisations link to their own pages, which list every story they appear in and what they have said. Before this links a story to earlier coverage of the same subject, including older background. Stories close after five quiet days, and later coverage opens a new story linked to the old one. Poly can now read a story and find related stories when you ask about the news.
