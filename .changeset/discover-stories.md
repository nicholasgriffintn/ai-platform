Discover now groups news into stories. When several outlets cover the same event, their articles join one story with a short overview, what the outlets agree and differ on, what is missing and how each outlet tells it. Open a news article in Discover and choose Full story to see it. The feed also shows one card per story, with the other outlets listed under it, instead of repeating the same event several times.
