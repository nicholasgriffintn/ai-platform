Let people reach Poly from their own chats. A personal Telegram or Slack binding pointed at Poly continues the Poly thread itself, with Poly's own tools and without navigation, and when a routine result interrupts, Poly also sends it to those bound chats through the idempotent outbound delivery record. Slack proactive messages post at the top of the conversation rather than into a thread.
