Poly now learns from the runs that go well and tells you which skills are earning their keep.

- **Skills drafted from your runs.** When a conversation you started finishes with five or more successful tool calls and you have not marked a reply down, Poly turns the sequence into a draft skill in your library and notes it on its agenda. Nothing goes live until you review and promote the draft.
- **Every skill shows how it is doing.** A skill's page now counts how often it was used, how many replies that used it were marked up or down, and how many corrections those conversations produced, with a plain verdict: new, healthy, unused or struggling.
- **A weekly nudge.** Each Monday Poly notes any personal skill that has gone unused for a month, or that keeps being marked down, so you can retire or revise it. It never retires anything itself.
