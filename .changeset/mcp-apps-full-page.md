Apps from your MCP servers now open on their own page. When a server you have connected offers a view as an app, it appears under Plugins in "Apps from your MCP servers" and opens full-page, in the same sandbox as views in a chat. It can read through your connection, and anything that would change something is still asked in a chat. A message the app sends lands in a new chat as a draft for you to send.
