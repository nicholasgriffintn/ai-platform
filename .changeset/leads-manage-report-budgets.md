A lead can now give its reports more budget when their work is worth it, up to what the workspace has left this month, and lower it when it isn't. Leads hear about a report nearing its cap before you do, so the 80% warning no longer lands in your Inbox for teammates that have a lead. Teammates paused at their budget also start again on their own at the start of a new month.
