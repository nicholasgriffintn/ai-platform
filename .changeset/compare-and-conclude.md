You can now compare any chat reply across models in one click. Choose **Compare and conclude** on a reply and two other models answer the same question on their own. Polychat shows where the answers agree and disagree, gives you one concluded answer, and shows what the comparison cost. The full answers are a click away, side by side.

Compare and conclude replaces the Second opinion button on chat replies. You can still ask for a second opinion in the conversation.
