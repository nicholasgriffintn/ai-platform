The now-playing controls no longer float over the bottom of the page, where they covered the composer. While audio plays somewhere you are not looking at its player, a Now playing row sits at the foot of the sidebar above Ask Poly, with a progress line, play and pause, stop, and a tap on the title to go back to the full player. When the sidebar is hidden, or on a phone, a compact play button with the title appears at the right of the page header instead.
