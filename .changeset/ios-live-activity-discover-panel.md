The iOS Live Activity now follows the agent run you care about: its title, what it is doing, how long it has been going and a button to jump in, plus a short "Completed" or "Failed" card when it finishes. Background jobs such as Discover writing an edition no longer keep it on your Lock Screen, and cancelled refreshes no longer show an error in the inbox.

Discover items on iPhone now open in a full panel like the web: step through the edition with the up and down arrows, save or tune a topic from the header, and ask about the item right there with suggested questions. Once you start chatting, the item folds into a compact row you can expand again.
