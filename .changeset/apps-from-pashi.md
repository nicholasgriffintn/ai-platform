Charades, Photo Sorter and Twenty Questions now come from Pashi instead of being built into Polychat. If you have linked Pashi, they appear in Plugins under "Apps from your MCP servers" and open as full-page apps, with nothing to do. If you have not, sign in to Polychat from pashi.app to link it and the games come back. Pashi's generators and converters are also reached through the linked Pashi server's tools now, so the Pashi Generator Toolkit recipe has been removed.
