Polis, Verbatim, Strudel, Charades, Photo Sorter and Twenty Questions now come from Pashi instead of being built into Polychat. If you have linked Pashi, they appear in your apps and open full-page, with nothing to do. If you have not, sign in to Polychat from pashi.app to link it and they come back. Shared Polis towns now open on pashi.app. In a chat, Poly writes, saves and lists Strudel patterns with Pashi's tools rather than a built-in one, and Pashi's generators and converters are reached the same way, so the Pashi Generator Toolkit recipe has been removed.
