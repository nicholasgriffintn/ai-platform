Your iPhone calendar, Apple Health and location now sync to Polychat and show up everywhere Discover does. Under iPhone data in Settings, turn on Calendar to add every calendar on your phone as a Discover source: your upcoming events appear on the Discover page and Poly plans around them, just like a connected Google Calendar. Apple Health now syncs sleep, steps, resting heart rate and heart rate variability through the day. Turn on Where I am for line status and weather near you, and allow location Always to keep it current as you move around.

Discover's Now feed also includes status for the lines you pick in Your day, plus lines near you. Three iPhone widgets show all of this from your Polychat feed: Today for what's next, Line status for your lines, and Health for your vitals compared with your usual.
