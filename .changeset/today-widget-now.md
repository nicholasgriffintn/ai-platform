The iPhone Today widget can now stand in for your calendar, health and tube widgets. In Settings, under Today widget, turn on your iPhone calendar to see your next event with a countdown, turn on health vitals for last night's sleep, today's steps and a note when your resting heart rate or heart rate variability is outside your usual range, and turn on Where I am for weather and line status near you. If you allow location Always, the widget keeps up as you move around, even when Polychat is closed, using low-power location updates. Your calendar and health stay on your phone. To choose which lines to watch, open Your day and go to Line status. Disruptions go to the top of the widget, and lines with good service are grouped into one row.
