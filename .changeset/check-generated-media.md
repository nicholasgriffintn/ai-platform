Images now open in the same viewer as your files, wherever you click them. Choose **File info** on a generated image, video or audio file and **Check this file** to see whether we have a record of it, including the model that made it.

To check a file someone sent you, open `/verify` and drop it in. It's checked on your device and only its fingerprint is sent. Screenshots and re-saved copies won't match, so no record doesn't mean a file isn't AI-generated.
