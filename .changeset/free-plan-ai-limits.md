Chatting now needs an account. Polychat-hosted models are part of Pro, and free accounts chat with their own provider keys or on-device models. Voice and semantic search are part of Pro too, and free accounts keep keyword search.

Models you choose for background work now stay in charge: if they fail, the job fails rather than quietly switching to a platform model. Official benchmarks no longer run on a schedule.
