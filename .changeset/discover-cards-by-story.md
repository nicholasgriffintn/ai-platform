Discover editions now show one card per story, like the live feed, with the number of outlets covering it. A topic whose articles mostly belong to one story shows that story's coverage straight away: what the outlets agree and differ on, how each tells it and what is missing. There is no longer a button to compare coverage, because the comparison is kept up to date as new outlets report. Discover can also bring back the earlier story behind something you read recently, as a "then and now" card.
