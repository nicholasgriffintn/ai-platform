Connect Pashi to your Polychat account. Approve Pashi in Polychat and it runs its AI on your plan and your default models, with a key that cannot reach your chats, projects or files. Polychat can then use Pashi tools for you, and on Pro that includes converting images, audio, video and documents from your chat. Connecting again replaces the old key, and you can revoke it from Profile or disconnect from Pashi. Device sign-in now tells you when a name comes from the device itself, so Polychat cannot vouch for it.
