Polychat now tells you that you are talking to an AI from the start of a conversation. The line sits under the composer on web and desktop, including the home screen, and appears on new chats on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. In Slack, Telegram, SMS and email, the first reply in a conversation opens with it.

Messages that mention suicide or self-harm now get support details at the top of the reply, whether or not guardrails are on. The details also appear when guardrails stop a message, and in connected channels when the assistant would otherwise stay quiet.
