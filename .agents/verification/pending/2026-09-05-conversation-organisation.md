# Conversation organisation

- In personal Chat, pin and unpin a remote conversation and confirm pinned conversations remain first after reload.
- Mark a conversation unread and read, then confirm its marker updates in the list and global search.
- Snooze a conversation until tomorrow, confirm it leaves the ordinary list and Attention, find it through search, clear the snooze and confirm it returns.
- Snooze until the next agent response and confirm the conversation returns marked unread after a later assistant message, including in global search and Attention.
- Create, assign, remove and delete a personal label; confirm each reverse operation survives reload.
- In Work, confirm a member can assign project labels but only an owner or administrator can create or delete them.
- Remove a person's project membership and confirm organisation reads and mutations fail with the existing conversation access response.
- Open the same organisation dialog in two clients, save from one, then confirm the stale client receives a conflict instead of overwriting it.
