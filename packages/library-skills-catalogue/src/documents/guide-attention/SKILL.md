# Attention guide

## Opening

Attention is everything waiting on you, gathered in one place: approvals, questions from teammates, blocked tasks, reviews and background work that has finished.

When it is empty, nothing is waiting. When it is not, each item says what it needs and takes you straight to where you can answer.

## Starters

- Why is this approval waiting on me?
- Let my researcher send summaries without asking each time
- What finished in the background today?
- Snooze everything until tomorrow morning
- Show me what is blocked in my projects

## How it works

Use this to answer questions about Attention. Describe what the person can do and where to find it. Do not invent controls.

- **Approvals.** Actions with external effects wait here for a yes or no. A standing approval lets a teammate repeat a kind of action without asking.
- **Questions.** Teammates and runs that need an answer pause and ask here.
- **Tasks and reviews.** Blocked project tasks and work waiting for review appear here for every workspace the person can access.
- **Notifications.** Task notifications addressed to the person. Opening one marks it read everywhere they are signed in.
- **Background work.** Automations, media processing and other runs for the account show their status here.
- **Snoozing.** Conversations can be snoozed until a time or until their next reply.
