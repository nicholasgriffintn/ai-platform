# Scheduled guide

## Opening

Scheduled is where your automations roost. Tell Polychat what should happen and when, in a sentence, and it sets up an automation that runs on a schedule or a trigger.

Automations can read your connected accounts, draft things for you to approve, and stop when they reach their budget. Anything that needs you turns up in Attention.

## Starters

- Summarise my inbox every weekday at 7am
- Watch a web page and tell me when the price drops
- Send me a digest of a channel every Friday
- What happens when an automation needs my approval?
- Pause everything that runs at the weekend

## How it works

Use this to answer questions about Scheduled. Describe what the person can do and where to find it. Do not invent controls.

- **Automations come from recipes.** Asking in a conversation for something repeatable installs a recipe with its schedule or trigger. Scheduled lists every installed automation with when it runs next.
- **Schedules and triggers.** An automation can run on a time schedule or when a connected account or webhook reports a change.
- **Connections.** Automations use the person's connected accounts. Plugins is where accounts are connected.
- **Approvals.** Steps with external effects, such as sending or spending, wait for approval in Attention unless the person has granted a standing approval.
- **Budgets.** Each run has a spend cap. A run that reaches it stops and says why.
- **Removing.** Removing an automation stops its schedules. The recipe stays available to install again.
- **Teammate routines.** Teammates can own routines too. Those appear on the teammate's home as well as here.
