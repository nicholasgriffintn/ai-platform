# Discover guide

## Opening

Discover is your morning edition. Each day Poly reads what you have been talking about, checks what is new on it and leaves a short brief, a few topics, links and the odd thing worth doing.

Pick a card to see why it is there, start a chat from it, or tell Discover you want more, less or none of that topic.

## Starters

- Why am I seeing this card?
- Cover the Japanese Grand Prix in tomorrow's edition
- Stop showing me football
- Write today's edition again
- Turn Discover off

## How it works

Use this to answer questions about Discover. Describe what the person can do and where to find it. Do not invent controls.

- **The edition.** Poly writes one edition each morning at 07:00 in the person's time zone. Write again in the page header asks for a fresh one now.
- **What it reads.** Recent personal chat titles, what Poly remembers when memory is on, feedback on earlier editions, anything they asked for, what is waiting on them and the web.
- **Listen.** A read card, and any text, web page or document in Files, has Listen, which reads it aloud in their narrator voice and starts with the first passage while the rest are recorded. On iPhone, a read card also has Share.
- **Cards.** Topics open a chat on today's angle, links open the page or a chat about it, things worth doing start the work in a chat, check-ins follow up on something they mentioned, and From before brings back missed flashcards or saved links. Every card says why it is there.
- **Sensitive topics.** Health, sexuality, religion, politics and money only appear when the person follows the topic or asked for it.
- **Watch and listen.** Today's episode, The Perch round-up, Wind down and, on Sundays, Your week all play in one player beside the feed. Tomorrow's episode is only made ahead when they watched past half of today's.
- **Wind down.** Opening Wind down suggests tonight's session from their day, last night's sleep and what they played recently. They can instead choose breathing, body scan, unwind (about their day, with a Mention my day switch), a slow story that continues over up to five nights, their Readwise highlights read back, park tomorrow (what they type comes back as things worth doing in the next edition) or just the sound. Each plays over a sound and an animated scene, with a slider between sound and voice and a Sound after menu that keeps the sound going for 15, 30 or 60 minutes. Evening editions can offer tonight's story under Worth a look, and it is only recorded when tapped.
- **Tidying.** Editions older than a fortnight are cleared unless a card was saved. Links Discover filed into a followed topic's notebook are removed after 30 days if they were never opened.
- **Feedback.** More like this, Less like this and Hide this topic shape the next edition. Hidden topics stop appearing.
- **Asking for something.** Settings has an Ask for something box. The next edition covers it.
- **Settings.** Settings in the page header has Tell me when it's ready, The Perch, episode length and style, and Connected services, where each connected service has its own switch for Discover. On iPhone, Discover can use yesterday's Apple Health sleep, mindful and exercise minutes for one line in the brief.
- **Turning it off.** Settings has a Morning edition switch. Earlier editions stay.
